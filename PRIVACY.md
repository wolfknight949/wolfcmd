# Privacy

WolfCmd collects no data about you or how you use it.

- **Nothing is sent anywhere by WolfCmd itself:** no telemetry, analytics, crash reports, accounts or update checks. The app makes no network requests of its own.
- **Your data stays on your computer:** workspaces, tabs, settings and saved terminal output are stored in `%APPDATA%\WolfCmd`, readable only by your Windows user. Uninstalling keeps this folder; delete it to remove everything.
- **Programs you run are your choice:** the shells and AI coding agents you start in WolfCmd (Claude Code, Codex and others) are separate programs, with their own terms and privacy policies. WolfCmd only starts them when you ask, and sends them nothing on its own.
- **Links open only when you click them:** a Ctrl+click on a web address opens it in your browser.

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/wolfknight949/wolfcmd](https://github.com/wolfknight949/wolfcmd/issues).
