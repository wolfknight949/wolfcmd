# @wolfcmd/ui-kit

**Purpose:** the design tokens and the few primitives the app uses, in WolfCmd's own look (N-01): **calm and warm**, the same on every OS.

## The look (N-01)
- **Structure:** a terminal-first window of blocks: panels are rounded blocks with a slim header, separated by gaps on the window background; a compact tab strip of pills on top; a slim widget bar on the right (icon over a small label).
- **Feel:** a warm neutral ramp of our own (warm dark greys, a warm off-white; never pure black or white), soft shapes (8 px controls, 12 px menus, dialogs and blocks, 6 px rows inside menus), soft diffuse shadows, generous room in dialogs, menus and settings, a calm type hierarchy (13 px base, larger medium-weight dialog headings).
- **Restraint:** monochrome-first chrome, thin quiet borders, simple line icons, one accent used sparingly (focus, selection, the primary button, the focused block): WolfCmd's light blue-violet (periwinkle).
- **WolfCmd's own details:** the focused block's border is the accent (`--wc-block-focus`), the one sign of where the keyboard is, and the workspace's color shows only as a small mark: the switcher's dot (no line on top of a block).
- **Motion (N-01):** short, calm and never flashy; only `transform` and `opacity` animate (no layout properties), every duration and easing comes from a token, and reduced motion zeroes the durations. Every control has a hover fill and a gentle press (`--wc-press-scale`: icon buttons, the bar's buttons, the switcher; `--wc-press-scale-wide`: text buttons and segments; a darker fill on menu rows and tab pills; the toggle's knob slides by a transform and squeezes while pressed). Things appear by fading and settling from `--wc-enter-scale` (dialogs with their scrim, tooltips, menus from their side, a pill's mark) and leave the same way (`useExitMotion`). Never on terminal content, never delaying input, never a timer while idle.
- **Per OS, only two things:** the system UI font (`--wc-font-ui`: Segoe UI on Windows, the San Francisco system font on macOS, the desktop's sans on Linux) and the window's buttons (the app's `chrome/title-bar.ts`: caption buttons on Windows and Linux, native traffic lights on macOS).

## Public API
- `styles.css` (import after `@import "tailwindcss";`): tokens (`--wc-*`: dark by default, Light under `data-theme="light"` on <html>, the compact density), base rules (no web-isms, the focus ring, slim scrollbars, our own radio buttons and checkboxes), motion, and the bundled icon font (`src/fonts/`; `wolfcmd-icons.json` lists its glyphs).
- `Button` (primary `accent`, quiet `standard`, ghost `subtle`, `danger`), `IconButton`, `Menu` (Radix dropdown with our metrics, keys and type-ahead, the shortcut column; N-10: `inset` items under the one before, a `title` tooltip; it leaves the focus to a dialog or anything else an item opened; N-02 closing, it gives the keyboard back at once to what had it before it opened (`menu-focus.ts`, `createFocusReturn`: the terminal, or the trigger when it was opened from there), never taking it from what an item or the user focused meanwhile, and a press on its trigger never takes it; N-06 `onCloseFocus` lets the caller place it, e.g. in a new panel; N-03 an item's own `contextMenu`, opened over the menu, which stays open), N-03 `ContextMenu` (`Menu`'s items and surface at a point, `at`/`onClose`; the focus goes back to what had it, as `Menu`'s does, unless an item moved it) with `menuPoint`, `isContextMenuKey` / `openContextMenuFrom` / `isKeyboardMenu` (Shift+F10 and the Menu key as a keyboard-marked `contextmenu`), `Dialog` (N-10: a modal dialog on a scrim, a form: focus moves in and is trapped (a radio group is one Tab stop), Esc closes, Enter submits, the focus goes back to what had it before the dialog rendered (or to `returnFocus`); N-07 `size: "wide"` for rows of settings), `TextField` (N-10: a text field with its label and one reserved line for a hint or an error), N-07 `ToggleSwitch` (a soft toggle, `role="switch"`, named by `aria-labelledby`), `Segmented` (one choice among a few, named by `labelledBy`: native radios, one Tab stop, arrows move; the chosen one a raised chip; `autoFocus` on the chosen one), `NumberBox` (a whole number in range with step buttons; arrows and Page Up/Down step, typing commits on Enter or blur), `modalOpen()` (a dialog is open: app shortcuts leave the keys to it), `Spinner` (N-06: a faint ring with a turning arc in the current color, `--wc-dur-spin` per turn, still under reduced motion), `TooltipHost` (the one tooltip for `title` attributes), N-01 motion helpers (`motion.ts`): `durationToken` / `easingToken` / `numberToken` (tokens for Web Animations on a ref; a duration is 0 under reduced motion), `useExitMotion(ref, className?)` (when its element unmounts, a copy fades out where it was: the dialog, the find bar, the paste question) and `leaveGhost(element, place)` (the copy itself: inert, `aria-hidden`, no role, id, title or `data-*` hook except `data-state`/`data-side`, removed after its exit; none under reduced motion), `GLYPHS` (icon names to code points), `cn`, `mixColors`, `parseDuration`, `chordLetter`, `isImeKey`, `isOneCharacter`, `typesAltGrCharacter`, N-02 `isApple` / `primaryModifier` (Cmd on macOS, Ctrl elsewhere) and `PlatformInfo`, N-03 `isWindows`.

## Icons
- `src/fonts/wolfcmd-icons.woff2` is a subset of **Lucide** (`lucide-static`, a root devDependency, ISC License; the Feather-derived icons MIT). `src/fonts/LICENSE` carries both notices; keep it next to the font.
- Glyphs sit at WolfCmd's own private-use code points (`GLYPHS`), so callers never change when the set does. A new icon: a line in `glyphs.ts` and one in `wolfcmd-icons.json` (code point → Lucide name), then `pnpm icons` (`tools/icon-font.mjs`, no other dependency) rebuilds the font.

## Token scales (`src/tokens/tokens.css`; the only values class lists may use)
ESLint's `wolfcmd/design-tokens` rule (`tools/eslint/design-tokens.js`, in `pnpm lint`) rejects pixel font sizes, opacity modifiers (`bg-fg/10`), raw colors, other radii and any bracketed value with a number (`w-[46px]`, `duration-[120ms]`). A value the scales lack becomes a token, used as `w-(--wc-caption-w)`.
- **Surfaces:** `bg-subtle` (the window, the title bar and the gaps between blocks), `bg` (a block), `bg-elevated` (raised: a chosen segment, a lifted pill), `flyout` (opaque menus and flyouts), `dialog-bg`, `term-bg`, `--wc-input-bg` (recessed fields), `--wc-tooltip-bg` / `-fg`. No surface is pure black or white.
- **Text:** `fg`, `fg-muted`, `fg-subtle` (meta, only on plain surfaces), `fg-disabled`; on accent: `accent-fg`.
- **Accent (one token):** `--wc-accent`, a light periwinkle in the dark theme (dark text on its fill, `--wc-accent-fg`) and a deeper shade of the same hue in the light theme, where it carries text, fills and the focus ring; `--wc-focus-ring` follows it. Everything else derives from it: `--wc-accent-hover` / `-pressed` (CSS), `accent-tint`, `accent-line`, `accent-fg-tint`, `--wc-block-focus`, `--wc-glow`.
- **Status:** `danger`, `warning`, `success` (text and icons), `--wc-danger-fill` / `-fill-fg` (a danger button), `warning-tint`, `warning-line`.
- **Fills and lines** (the only see-through colors): `fill-quiet`, `fill-hover`, `fill-pressed`, `fill-strong`, `line`, `line-strong`, `scrim`; controls: `--wc-button-bg` / `-hover` / `-pressed` (the quiet secondary button), `--wc-input-border` / `-hover`, `--wc-surface-border` (overlays), `--wc-divider`.
- **N-06 glow:** a tab pill's one glow when an agent finished unseen (`.wc-glow` with `data-glowing`, opacity only, eased by `--wc-ease-gentle`, `--wc-dur-glow` 1.2 s): in the finished agent's color when the app sets `--wc-glow-agent` inline with `data-agent` (a ring and tint in it, a halo of its luminous tint: the color mixed toward `--wc-finish-glow-lift`), else `--wc-glow` (an accent ring, a halo with a touch of the warm tone `--wc-glow-warm`, a tint).
- **N-06 finish glow (a panel's block):** `.wc-finish-glow` (with `.wc-finish-glow-haze` and `-line`; played by `data-glowing`), placed inside a block card (absolute, inside its border, its rounded corners): a short haze fading inward from the block's edge and a thin bright line on it swell up slowly and fade out softly, one breath; nothing moves around the block. Only the agent's color: `--wc-finish-glow-agent` (set inline by the app; else a quiet neutral) and its luminous tint (the agent's color mixed toward `--wc-finish-glow-lift`, keeping `--wc-finish-glow-tint-mix` of it: more in the light theme, so it reads on a light block). Also `--wc-finish-glow-w` (how far the haze reaches in), `--wc-finish-glow-line`, `--wc-finish-glow-strength` (the haze's opacity, stronger in the light theme) and `--wc-dur-finish-glow` (2.8 s, eased by `--wc-ease-gentle`). Hidden and still while idle; opacity only (its masks are static); none under reduced motion or forced colors.
- **Workspace colors:** `--wc-workspace-1` … `-8`, small marks only (the switcher's dot, an agent's dot); deeper in the light theme.
- **Spacing:** Tailwind's numbers on a 4 px step (`p-2` = 8 px; `--wc-space-1`…`-8`).
- **Sizes:** `--wc-titlebar-h`, `--wc-tab-h`, `--wc-tab-w` / `--wc-tab-min-w` (pills), `--wc-header-h` (block headers), `--wc-widgetbar-w` / `--wc-widget-w` / `--wc-widget-label` (the right bar), `--wc-caption-w`, `--wc-traffic-lights-w` (macOS), `--wc-panel-gap`, `--wc-control-h` / `-sm` (fields, buttons, pickers), N-07 `--wc-toggle-*`, `--wc-numberbox-w`, `--wc-dialog-wide-max-w`, `--wc-field-w` (a field or picker in a settings row), `--wc-findbar-max-w` / `--wc-panel-overlay-max-h` (overlays inside a panel), menu, tooltip and dialog metrics. The compact density (`data-density="compact"` below 1280 px) shrinks them.
- **Radii:** `rounded-control` (8 px), `rounded-overlay` (12 px), `rounded-full`, `rounded-none`; `--wc-radius-item` (6 px) for rows inside menus and segments (CSS only).
- **Type:** `text-xs` 12 · `text-sm` 13 (the UI's base) · `text-md` 14 · `text-base` 15 · `text-heading` 16 · `text-lg` 20, each with its line height; icons `text-icon-xs` 10 … `text-icon-xl` 24. Weights: normal, medium, semibold.
- **Elevation:** `shadow-flyout`, `shadow-dialog` (and the tooltip's): soft and diffuse, lighter in the light theme.
- **Motion:** `--wc-dur-fast` 100 ms (hovers, presses, exits), `--wc-dur-normal` 160 ms (entrances, a new panel or pill, the focus border), `--wc-dur-slow` 240 ms (maximize and restore), N-06 `--wc-dur-glow` 1.2 s and `--wc-dur-finish-glow` 2.8 s (with `--wc-ease-gentle`, a sine-like curve), `--wc-dur-spin` (all 0 under reduced motion), `ease-out`, `ease-in-out`, `--wc-delay-hover`, `--wc-drag-dim`, `--wc-lift-scale`, N-01 `--wc-press-scale` (0.96) and `--wc-press-scale-wide` (0.98) for a press, `--wc-enter-scale` (0.97) for something appearing, `--wc-flyout-slide` (a 4 px drift). Classes: `wc-press`, `wc-flyout-in`, `wc-fade-in`, `wc-mark-in`, `wc-notice-in`, `wc-dialog-in`, `wc-scrim-in`, `wc-leaving` (+ `wc-leaving-fade`).

## Invariants
- Only token colors exist (Tailwind's palette is cleared); two radii; the type and icon scales are the only text sizes. Components use tokens, never raw values (the ESLint rule above).
- N-01: every text, status and icon color keeps WCAG contrast on the surfaces it is drawn on, in both themes (`src/tokens/contrast.test.ts`: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 icons and focus rings).
- `--wc-bg-subtle` is also the native window's background: `DARK_BACKGROUND` / `LIGHT_BACKGROUND` in `crates/wcmd-app/src/startup.rs` and `backgroundColor` in `tauri.conf.json` / `tauri.macos.conf.json` must match it.
- Flyouts and menus are opaque; a closing one (its 100 ms fade-out) ignores the pointer, and so does an exit copy (`wc-leaving`).
- Every `GLYPHS` code point is in the font manifest and the bundled WOFF2, and every manifest name is a Lucide icon (a test checks).
- No dependency on `@wolfcmd/app` or the bindings.
- Menus and dialogs register in `overlays.ts` so Esc closes the one on top and `modalOpen()` knows about a dialog.
